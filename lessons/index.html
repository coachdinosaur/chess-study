Recommended course path

Start with the rules and basic mates, continue into tactics and practical draw technique, then move into disciplined calculation and intermediate positional play.

Level 1

Pawn Level

5 modules · 55 lessons · Beginner

Board knowledge, notation, piece movement, check and checkmate, basic mating technique, castling, and en passant.

Open Pawn Level →Level 2

Advanced Pawn / Pawn Plus

10 modules · 116 lessons · Bridge level

Mating patterns, basic tactics, sacrifices, draw rules, stalemate technique, repetition, pawn structure, and chess etiquette.

Open Advanced Pawn →Level 3

Bishop Level

13 modules · 17 lessons · Intermediate

Thinking principles, technical checkmates, calculation, weaknesses, tactical exchanges, defense, positional evaluation, guided FEN conversion, and positional planning.

Open Bishop Level →

Suggested order: complete Pawn Level first, use Advanced Pawn as the transition course, then begin Bishop Level. Students may revisit earlier modules whenever a weakness appears, which is considerably more sensible than pretending learning only moves forward.
