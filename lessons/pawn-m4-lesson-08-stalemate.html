What Is Stalemate?

A stalemate happens when the player whose turn it is has no legal moves, but is not in check.

Stalemate means: No legal moves + not in check.

When stalemate happens, the game is a draw. It is a tie. Both players get half a point.

Stalemate is not checkmate! In stalemate, the game ends in a draw. In checkmate, the game is a win for the player delivering checkmate.
2

Stalemate Example 1

Black to move.

White: King a1, Queen b6. Black: King a8.

FEN: k7/8/1Q6/8/8/8/8/K7 b - - 0 1

The White queen on b6 controls every possible square that the Black king can move to. This is stalemate if it is Black's turn to move.

3

Stalemate Example 2

Black to move.

White: King c3, Queen d3. Black: King c1.

FEN: 8/8/8/8/8/2KQ4/8/2k5 b - - 0 1

With Black to play, the position is stalemate. Even if the White king is on a3 or b3, it is still stalemate. The White king guards the b2 square.

A common mistake is confusing stalemate with checkmate. Remember: in stalemate, the king is not in check; in checkmate, the king is in check and cannot escape.

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding.

Is stalemate a win?

No. Stalemate is a draw.

What two things must be true for stalemate?

The player has no legal moves and is not in check.

How many points do players get after a stalemate?

Each player gets half a point.
