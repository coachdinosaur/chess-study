How Do You Win a Chess Game?

To win a chess game, you must checkmate the king. Checkmate means the enemy king is in check and has no safe way to escape.

Know checkmateUnderstand that checkmate wins the game.
Check firstThe enemy king must be under attack.
No escapeThe king cannot capture, block, or run away.
Game overOnce checkmate happens, the game is finished.

Module Goal

Module 3 teaches the important words and ideas needed to understand how a chess game is won. This lesson focuses on checkmate.

Lesson focus
You win a chess game by checkmating the enemy king. A checkmate happens when the king is checked and cannot escape.
2

What Is Checkmate?

A checkmate means the enemy king is completely trapped.

1
The king is in check
One of your pieces is attacking the enemy king.
2
The king has no escape
The checked king cannot get out of danger.
3
You win the game
If the king is checked and cannot escape, it is checkmate.
Checkmate = check + no way out.
3

The Enemy King Cannot

When all three escape choices are impossible, the position is checkmate.

✕

Capture

The king cannot capture the checking piece.

✕

Run away

The king cannot move to a safe square.

✕

Block

No piece can hide the king from the check.

If capture, running away, and blocking are all impossible, the game is won by checkmate.
4

Diagram

The black bishop on d5 checks the white king on h1. The escape squares g1, g2, and h2 are not available, so the white king has no way out.

✕
White cannot capture the bishop
The bishop is too far away from the white king.
✕
No piece can block the bishop
There is no white piece that can move in between the bishop and the king.
✕
The white king cannot run away
The nearby escape squares are all controlled.
This is: CHECKMATE!
Once the king is checkmated, the game is over.
5

Remember

Use this short checklist when deciding if a position is checkmate.

CheckThe enemy king must be under attack.
No captureThe king or another piece cannot capture the checking piece.
No blockNo piece can block the line of attack.
No safe squareThe king cannot run away to a safe square.
WinIf there is check and no escape, it is checkmate.
Game overThe game ends immediately after checkmate.
✓

Review Questions

Use these quick questions at the end of the lesson.

1. How do you win a chess game?

You win by checkmating the enemy king.

2. What is checkmate?

Checkmate means the king is in check and has no legal way to escape.

3. What three escape ideas must fail for checkmate?

The king cannot capture the checking piece, run away, or block the check.
