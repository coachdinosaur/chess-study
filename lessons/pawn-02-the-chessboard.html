The Chessboard

The chessboard has 64 squares. These squares are divided by files, ranks, and diagonals. Before a game begins, remember: White on the right!

The 64-Square Board

A chessboard is made of 64 squares. It has 8 squares across and 8 squares up and down.

Main idea
8 files × 8 ranks = 64 squares. Every chess move happens on one of these squares.
2

Alternating Colors

The board has alternating light and dark squares. This pattern helps players see lines, diagonals, and piece movement more clearly.

8×8

Grid

The chessboard is always an 8 by 8 grid.

▦

Light and dark

The colors alternate from one square to the next.

h1

Correct corner

From White’s side, the bottom-right square is h1 and should be light.

3

Files

Files are the vertical columns of the chessboard. They go up and down.

File names

Files are named with lowercase letters:

  • a-file
  • b-file
  • c-file
  • d-file
  • e-file
  • f-file
  • g-file
  • h-file
Example
The squares a1, a2, a3, a4, a5, a6, a7, and a8 are all on the a-file.
4

Ranks

Ranks are the horizontal rows of the chessboard. They go from left to right.

Rank numbers

Ranks are numbered:

  • 1st rank
  • 2nd rank
  • 3rd rank
  • 4th rank
  • 5th rank
  • 6th rank
  • 7th rank
  • 8th rank
Example
The squares a1, b1, c1, d1, e1, f1, g1, and h1 are all on the 1st rank.
5

Diagonals

Diagonals are connected squares that run slanting across the board. Diagonal squares are always the same color.

Example 1The squares a1, b2, c3, d4, e5, f6, g7, and h8 form one long diagonal.
Example 2The squares h1, g2, f3, e4, d5, c6, b7, and a8 form another long diagonal.
6

White on the Right

When you face the chessboard, the bottom-right square should be a light square.

Remember
Before beginning a game, check the board: White on the right! That means the bottom-right square should always be light.

Review Questions

Use these quick questions at the end of the lesson.

1. How many squares are on a chessboard?

A chessboard has 64 squares.

2. What are files?

Files are the vertical columns of the board. They are named a to h.

3. What are ranks?

Ranks are the horizontal rows of the board. They are numbered 1 to 8.

4. What are diagonals?

Diagonals are connected squares that run slanting across the board.

5. What does “White on the right” mean?

It means the bottom-right square should be a light square before the game begins.
