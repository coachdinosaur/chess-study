The Chessmen

Learn how many chessmen are used in a standard game, how they are divided between White and Black, and which chessmen are pieces or pawns.

32 chessmenA standard game begins with 32 total chessmen.
16 eachWhite has 16 chessmen and Black has 16 chessmen.
Pieces and pawnsA chessman is either a piece or a pawn.
Player inventoryEach player has the same starting set of chessmen.
32 chessmen in one game
White has 16. Black has 16. Each side begins with the same army.
Lesson contents
1. All Chessmen 2. Important Facts 3. Piece or Pawn 4. White Chessmen 5. Black Chessmen 6. Each Player Has 7. Summary 8. Review Questions
1

All Chessmen in One Game

A standard chess game begins with 32 chessmen: 16 for White and 16 for Black.

Use this overview to show that both players start with equal material.

2

Important Facts

Students should remember these three counts before studying piece movement.

32
Total chessmenThere are 32 chessmen in one game.
16
White chessmenWhite has 16 chessmen.
16
Black chessmenBlack has 16 chessmen.
Teacher note
In this lesson, the word piece means king, queen, rook, knight, or bishop. Pawns are counted separately as pawns.
3

Piece or Pawn?

A chessman can be a piece or a pawn.

Pieces

The pieces are the king, queen, rooks, knights, and bishops.

Pawns

Pawns are chessmen too, but they are named separately from the pieces.

4

White Has 16 Chessmen

White begins with one complete army.

5

Black Has 16 Chessmen

Black begins with the same number of chessmen as White.

6

Lesson Summary

Use this checklist to remember the chessmen in one game.

1 kingEach player has one king.
1 queenEach player has one queen.
2 rooksEach player has two rooks.
2 knightsEach player has two knights.
2 bishopsEach player has two bishops.
8 pawnsEach player has eight pawns.
Main idea
Each player has 1 king, 1 queen, 2 rooks, 2 knights, 2 bishops, and 8 pawns.
8

Review Questions

Check that students understand the counts and categories.

How many chessmen are there in one game?

There are 32 chessmen.

How many chessmen does White have?

White has 16 chessmen.

How many chessmen does Black have?

Black has 16 chessmen.

Is a pawn counted as a piece in this lesson?

No. A chessman is either a piece or a pawn. Pieces are king, queen, rook, knight, and bishop.

How many pawns does each player have?

Each player has 8 pawns.
