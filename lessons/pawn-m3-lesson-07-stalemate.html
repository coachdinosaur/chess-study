Stalemate

A stalemate happens when the player to move is not in check, but has no legal move. Stalemate is a draw.

Module Goal

Module 3 teaches the words needed to understand how a chess game ends. This lesson explains stalemate.

Lesson focus
Stalemate is different from checkmate. In checkmate, the king is in check. In stalemate, the king is not in check, but the player has no legal move.
2

Stalemate vs Checkmate

Both positions can trap a king, but the result is different.

Checkmate

The king is in check and has no escape. The game is won.

Stalemate

The king is not in check, but the player has no legal move. The game is a draw.

Stalemate means the game is a draw.
3

Important Ideas

Use this three-part test to recognize stalemate.

1
It is White to move
In the diagram position, it is White’s turn.
2
The king is not in check
The white king is not being attacked on its current square.
3
There is no legal move
The king cannot move safely, and White has no other legal move.
If you have no legal move and your king is not in check, it is stalemate.

Diagram

White to move. The white king on h1 is not in check, but it cannot move to g1, g2, or h2. White has no legal move.

✕
The white king is not in check
The black queen does not attack h1.
✕
The white king cannot run or hide
All nearby squares are controlled by Black.
✕
White has no legal move
So the position is a stalemate.
This is: STALEMATE!
5

Tournament Scoring

A stalemate is a draw. That means no one won and no one lost.

Result Points
Win1 point
Loss0 points
Draw1/2 point
Stalemate = draw = 1/2 point for each player.
6

Remember

Use this short checklist when deciding if a position is stalemate.

Not in checkThe king is not being attacked on its current square.
No legal moveThe player to move cannot make any legal move.
DrawA stalemate is not a win. It is a draw.
CompareCheckmate wins because the king is in check and cannot escape.
ScoreIn tournament scoring, each player receives 1/2 point for a draw.
WarningDo not accidentally stalemate when you are trying to checkmate.

Review Questions

Use these quick questions at the end of the lesson.

1. What is stalemate?

Stalemate happens when the player to move is not in check but has no legal move.

2. Is stalemate a win?

No. Stalemate is a draw.

3. What is the difference between checkmate and stalemate?

In checkmate, the king is in check and has no escape. In stalemate, the king is not in check but has no legal move.
