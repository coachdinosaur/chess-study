Lesson Goal

In this lesson, you will learn how the King moves on the chessboard.

Key idea
The King can move in any direction, but only one square at a time.
2

The King

The King is the leader of your chess army. The King is the most important piece in chess, but it is also one of the slowest pieces on the chessboard.

Why the King matters

Every move in chess must keep your King safe. If your King is checkmated, you lose the game.

Most important piece: King
Normal movement: one square
3

The King Can Move in Any Direction

The King can move one square forward, backward, left, right, or diagonally.

ForwardOne square toward the opponent’s side.
BackwardOne square toward your own side.
LeftOne square to the left.
RightOne square to the right.
DiagonalOne square slanting in any diagonal direction.
The King is powerful because it can move in every direction, but it is slow because it moves only one square.

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding.

1. What is the most important piece in chess?

The King is the most important piece in chess.

2. How many squares can the King move at a time?

The King can move one square at a time.

3. Can the King move diagonally?

Yes. The King can move one square diagonally.

4. If the King is on e4, can it move to e5?

Yes. e5 is one square forward from e4.

5. What happens if your King is checkmated?

You lose the game.
