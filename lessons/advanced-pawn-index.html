Coach Dinosaur Chess Course

Advanced Pawn Level

A bridge between Pawn Level and Bishop Level. Learn practical mating patterns, tactical sacrifices, king restriction, and accurate finishing technique.

Course placement: harder than Pawn Level, but deliberately more accessible than Bishop / Intermediate Level. Module 1 contains 49 short lessons and 53 FEN positions.
Module 1Checkmate Patterns49 lessons · 53 FEN positions
  1. 1.The Box Mate

    A box mate uses a rook or queen to restrict the enemy king inside a shrinking box. Includes 6 FEN positions.

  2. 2.Avoiding Stalemate in the Box Mate

    Do not shrink the box automatically. Includes 1 FEN position.

  3. 3.General Mate vs. the Lone King

    When the opponent has only a king, you must know which material can force checkmate.

  4. 4.Two Knights vs. King

    Two knights cannot force checkmate against a lone king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  5. 5.Back Rank Mate 1

    A back-rank mate occurs when a king is trapped on its home rank, usually by its own pawns, and a rook or queen gives check along that rank. Includes 1 FEN position.

  6. 6.Back Rank Mate 2

    Back-rank weaknesses often allow a forcing sacrifice. Includes 1 FEN position.

  7. 7.Morphy's Mate 1

    Morphy's Mate is named after Paul Morphy. Includes 1 FEN position.

  8. 8.Morphy's Mate 2

    This fuller example shows the same rook-and-bishop coordination in a tactical position. Includes 1 FEN position.

  9. 9.Epaulette Mate

    In an epaulette mate, the king's two neighbouring flight squares on the same rank are occupied by its own pieces, commonly two rooks. Includes 1 FEN position.

  10. 10.Blackburne's Mate 1

    Blackburne's Mate is a rare pattern involving two bishops and a knight against a castled king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  11. 11.Blackburne's Mate 2

    The attacking idea begins with a sacrifice that opens the king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  12. 12.Arabian Mate 1

    The Arabian Mate uses a rook and knight. Includes 1 FEN position.

  13. 13.Arabian Mate 2

    When setting up an Arabian Mate, watch for stalemate. Includes 1 FEN position.

  14. 14.Anastasia's Mate 1

    Anastasia's Mate uses a knight to cover the king's escape squares, followed by a rook check along the edge file or rank. Includes 1 FEN position.

  15. 15.Anastasia's Mate 2

    This tactical version uses the same knight-and-rook pattern but requires a forcing sequence. Includes 1 FEN position.

  16. 16.Hook Mate 1

    The Hook Mate uses a rook, knight, and pawn, often together with an enemy pawn that blocks the king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  17. 17.Hook Mate 2

    This advanced Hook Mate begins with an underpromotion. Includes 1 FEN position.

  18. 18.Anderssen's Mate 1

    In Anderssen's Mate, a rook or queen gives the decisive check while the defending king is trapped near the edge by its own pawns and the attacking pieces. Includes 1 FEN position.

  19. 19.Anderssen's Mate 2

    A second example of the same edge-of-board mating geometry. Includes 1 FEN position.

  20. 20.Boden's Mate 1

    Boden's Mate is delivered by two bishops on crossing diagonals. Includes 1 FEN position.

  21. 21.Boden's Mate 2

    This position requires a sacrifice before the bishops can finish the attack. Includes 1 FEN position.

  22. 22.Fool's Mate 1

    Fool's Mate is the fastest possible checkmate from the initial position. Includes 1 FEN position.

  23. 23.Fool's Mate 2

    This exercise presents a different tactical position built around the same exposed-king idea. Includes 1 FEN position.

  24. 24.Reti's Mate

    Reti's Mate uses a bishop and rook against a king whose own pieces severely restrict its movement. Includes 1 FEN position.

  25. 25.Corner Mate 1

    In a Corner Mate, the king is trapped in the corner. Includes 1 FEN position.

  26. 26.Corner Mate 2

    Before delivering the knight mate, White must remove or deflect the piece that can capture the checking knight. Includes 1 FEN position.

  27. 27.Smothered Mate 1

    A smothered mate occurs when a king is completely surrounded by its own pieces and a knight gives checkmate. Includes 1 FEN position.

  28. 28.Smothered Mate 2

    A smothered mate does not always require a pin. Includes 1 FEN position.

  29. 29.Pillsbury's Mate 1

    Pillsbury's Mate occurs when a bishop controls the corner square next to a castled king and a rook gives mate on the open file. Includes 1 FEN position.

  30. 30.Pillsbury's Mate 2

    This version combines the mating pattern with sacrifices. Includes 1 FEN position.

  31. 31.Max Lange's Mate 1

    In a Max Lange Mate, the defending king is restricted by its own pawns. Includes 1 FEN position.

  32. 32.Max Lange's Mate 2

    White must first reposition the bishop with forcing play, then complete the queen-and-bishop mating pattern. Includes 1 FEN position.

  33. 33.Opera Mate 1

    The Opera Mate uses a rook and bishop attacking a common mating square on the edge of the board. Includes 1 FEN position.

  34. 34.Opera Mate 2

    A tactical example in which White must force the rook-and-bishop mating arrangement. Includes 1 FEN position.

  35. 35.Legal's Mate

    Legal's Mate is delivered by two knights and a bishop, usually after the opponent accepts a queen sacrifice. Includes 1 FEN position.

  36. 36.Scholar's Mate

    Scholar's Mate uses the queen and bishop to attack f7. Includes 1 FEN position.

  37. 37.Greco's Mate 1

    Greco's Mate is delivered by a rook or queen while a bishop controls one escape square and the defending king's own pawn blocks another. Includes 1 FEN position.

  38. 38.Greco's Mate 2

    A tactical position that must be converted into the Greco mating pattern. Includes 1 FEN position.

  39. 39.Blind Swine Mate

    Two connected rooks on the seventh rank, or the second rank for Black, repeatedly check the king and often force mate. Includes 1 FEN position.

  40. 40.Damiano's Mate 1

    Damiano's Mate occurs when a queen, supported by a pawn, mates a king on the edge of the board. Includes 1 FEN position.

  41. 41.Damiano's Mate 2

    This example reaches Damiano's Mate through a long series of rook sacrifices that drag the king onto the mating square. Includes 1 FEN position.

  42. 42.Swallow-Tail Mate

    Also called the Gueridon Mate, the Swallow-Tail Mate is delivered by a queen directly in front of the king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  43. 43.Triangle Mate

    The Triangle Mate uses a queen and rook. Includes 1 FEN position.

  44. 44.Dovetail Mate

    Also called Cozio's Mate, the Dovetail Mate occurs when the king's own pawns block its backward diagonal escapes. Includes 1 FEN position.

  45. 45.Lolli's Mate

    Lolli's Mate is a queen-and-pawn mating pattern, usually against a castled king. Includes 1 FEN position.

  46. 46.Killbox Mate

    The Killbox Mate uses a queen and rook to confine and mate a king on the side or edge of the board. Includes 1 FEN position.

  47. 47.H-file Mate

    The H-file Mate is built by opening the h-file with a rook sacrifice, continuing with forcing checks, and placing the queen on h5 or h-file squares for an unavoidable mate. Includes 1 FEN position.

  48. 48.Bishop-Queen Mate 1

    A basic queen-and-bishop battery can produce direct mating threats and many tactical combinations. Includes 1 FEN position.

  49. 49.Bishop-Queen Mate 2

    The mating threat can also create tactical opportunities elsewhere. Includes 1 FEN position.
