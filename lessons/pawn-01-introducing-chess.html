Introducing the Game of Chess

What chess is, where it came from, and why people all over the world love playing it.

In this lesson you will learn:

  • What chess is and why it matters
  • Where chess came from
  • How the modern game developed
  • Why chess is worth learning
1

One of the World's Oldest Games

Chess is one of the oldest games in the world. It has been played for centuries and is still popular today. Millions of people play chess online and in tournaments across the globe.

A Journey Through Time

Chess has traveled across continents and centuries to become the game we know today.

Chess began in India around 600 AD as a game called chaturanga.

The game spread to Persia where it became shatranj. Arabic scholars wrote the first strategy books.

Chess arrived in Europe through trade routes and became a favorite pastime of kings and nobles.

The modern rules were born in Southern Europe. The queen and bishop gained their powerful moves.

Wilhelm Steinitz became the first official World Chess Champion in 1886.

Why Learn Chess?

Chess has become a symbol of intelligence and strategic thinking. But it is not just for geniuses — it is easy to learn and fun to play at every level.

World Champions

The first World Championship was held in 1886. Many great champions have enjoyed and loved the game of chess, and we hope that you will too!
