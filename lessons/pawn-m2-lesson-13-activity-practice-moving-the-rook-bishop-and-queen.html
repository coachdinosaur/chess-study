1

Activity Goal

Practice moving the Rook, Bishop, and Queen correctly.

Key idea
The Rook moves straight, the Bishop moves diagonally, and the Queen moves both ways.
2

Set Up a Simple Board Position

Use one White King, one Black King, one Rook, one Bishop, and one Queen.

White

  • King: e1
  • Queen: d1
  • Rook: a1
  • Bishop: c1

Black

  • King: e8
3

Activity Board

Place the pieces on the board exactly as shown.

Practice setup

This simple setup lets you practice the Rook, Bishop, and Queen without extra pieces blocking the board.

4

Practice Tasks

Work through these tasks with your coach.

1
Move the Rook in straight lines

Use files and ranks only. Do not move diagonally.

2
Move the Bishop diagonally

The Bishop must stay on diagonal squares.

3
Move the Queen straight and diagonally

The Queen combines the Rook and Bishop moves.

4
Practice captures

Capture only when an enemy piece is on a legal square and no piece blocks the path.

5

Movement Examples

Rook examples

Bishop examples

Queen examples

6

Remember

Before moving, name the piece and name the movement rule.

RookStraight lines: up, down, left, right.
BishopDiagonal lines only.
QueenStraight lines and diagonal lines.
No jumpingRook, Bishop, and Queen cannot jump over pieces.
✓

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding.

1. How does the Rook move?

The Rook moves in straight lines: up, down, left, or right.

2. How does the Bishop move?

The Bishop moves diagonally.

3. How does the Queen move?

The Queen moves in straight lines and diagonally.

4. Can the Rook, Bishop, or Queen jump over pieces?

No. They cannot jump over pieces.

5. When is a capture legal?

A capture is legal when the enemy piece is on a square your piece can legally move to and no piece blocks the path.
