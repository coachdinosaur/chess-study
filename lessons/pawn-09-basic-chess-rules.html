Basic Chess Rules

Learn the first rules of a standard chess game: two players, taking turns, White moving first, making a move on your turn, and the correct starting position.

Two playersOne side controls White, and the other side controls Black.
Turn orderWhite moves, then Black moves, then the turns continue.
White startsWhite always makes the first move in standard chess.
Starting positionWhite begins on ranks 1–2; Black begins on ranks 7–8.
↔
Two players. One move at a time.
White moves first, Black replies, and every turn must be a legal move.

White Moves First

At the beginning of every standard chess game, the first move belongs to White.

You Must Move

When it is your turn, you must make a move. You cannot skip or pass your turn in chess.

4

When it is your turn, you must make a legal move.

No passing turns: every turn requires a legal move.

If it is your turn, choose and play one legal move.
5

The Starting Position

The starting position of standard chess has White pieces on the 1st and 2nd ranks, and Black pieces on the 7th and 8th ranks.

White pieces: 1st and 2nd ranks Black pieces: 7th and 8th ranks White always moves first

Basic Rule Summary

Use these simple rules before starting full games and notation practice.

Two playersWhite and Black play against each other.
Take turnsOnly one side moves on each turn.
White startsWhite always moves first.
No skippingOn your turn, you must make a legal move.
White setupWhite begins on ranks 1 and 2.
Black setupBlack begins on ranks 7 and 8.
Teacher note
Keep this lesson simple. Detailed legal-move rules, check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, and special moves can be taught in later lessons.
7

Review Questions

Check that students understand the most basic flow of a chess game.

How many players play a chess game?

Two players: one controls White and one controls Black.

Who moves first in standard chess?

White always moves first.

Can a player skip a turn?

No. When it is your turn, you must make a legal move.

Where do the white pieces start?

White pieces start on the 1st and 2nd ranks.

Where do the black pieces start?

Black pieces start on the 7th and 8th ranks.
