Rook Mate Example 1

White uses the shrinking box method to push the Black king toward the edge of the board.

Shrinking boxLearn how the rook cuts off the king.
King supportLearn when the king should move closer.
Good movesUnderstand the meaning of !.

1

Starting Position

The first step is to force the Black king to the side of the board. We call this the shrinking box method.

White to move. White: king g2, rook f1. Black: king d5.

2

First Box

Checks do not always help drive the king to the edge. After Rf4, the Black king is trapped in a box.

Move: 1. Rf4!

3

Make the Box Smaller

The box has shrunk to 16 squares.

1. Rf4! Ke5 2. Kf3 Kd5 3. Re4

4

Move the King When Needed

The symbol ! means a good move. If White cannot make the box smaller with the rook, the king moves forward and guards the rook.

3... Kc5 4. Ke3 Kd5 5. Kd3!

5

Twelve Squares

Only 12 squares now.

5... Kc5 6. Rd4

6

Eight Squares

The Black king is down to 8 squares.

6... Kb5 7. Rc4

7

Four Squares

Only 4 squares are left in the box.

7... Kb6 8. Kd4 Kb5 9. Kd5 Kb6 10. Rc5 Kb7 11. Rc6

8

Possible Finish

The symbol # means checkmate.

11... Kb8 12. Kc5 Kb7 13. Kb5 Ka7

If 13... Kb8, White mates in two: 14. Kb6 Ka8 15. Rc8#

If 13... Ka8, White wins by: 14. Rc7 Kb8 15. Kb6 Ka8 16. Rc8#

The # symbol means checkmate!
✓

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding.

What is the shrinking box method?

It is a method where the rook cuts off the enemy king and gradually makes the king's space smaller.

What does ! mean?

A good move.

What does # mean?

Checkmate.
