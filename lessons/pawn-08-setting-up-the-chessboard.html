Setting Up the Chessboard

Learn how to place each chess piece on its correct starting square: kings, queens, rooks, knights, bishops, and pawns.

Board directionRemember “White on the right.”
Kings and queensPlace the central pieces correctly.
Back rank orderRook, knight, bishop, queen, king, bishop, knight, rook.
PawnsWhite on rank 2, black on rank 7.

Set up correctly
White on the right. Queen on her own color. Pawns in front.

1

Before placing the pieces

Start with the board facing the correct direction, then build the starting position step by step.

White on the rightThe bottom-right square from White’s side is h1, and it must be light.
Queen on her own colorThe white queen starts on the light square d1. The black queen starts on the dark square d8.
2

Place the Kings

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the kings on their starting squares.

  • White king: e1
  • Black king: e8

The white king starts on e1, a dark square.

The black king starts on e8, a light square.

3

Place the Queens

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the queens on their starting squares.

  • White queen: d1
  • Black queen: d8

The white queen starts on d1, a light square.

The black queen starts on d8, a dark square.

Easy memory trick: the queens start on their own color.

4

Place the Rooks

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the rooks on the corners of the board.

  • White rooks: a1, h1
  • Black rooks: a8, h8

The rooks begin on the four corner squares.

5

Place the Knights

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the knights beside the rooks.

  • White knights: b1, g1
  • Black knights: b8, g8
6

Place the Bishops

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the bishops beside the knights.

  • White bishops: c1, f1
  • Black bishops: c8, f8
7

Place the Pawns

Follow this board diagram and place the pieces on the named starting squares.

What to do

Place the pawns in front of the other pieces.

  • White pawns: a2, b2, c2, d2, e2, f2, g2, h2
  • Black pawns: a7, b7, c7, d7, e7, f7, g7, h7

Now the chessboard is fully set up and ready for play.

8

Remember

Use these rules to check the setup before starting a game.

White on the rightThe square h1 is light from White’s side.
Queens on their colorWhite queen on d1; black queen on d8.
Back rank orderRook, knight, bishop, queen, king, bishop, knight, rook.
Pawns in frontWhite pawns start on rank 2; black pawns start on rank 7.

9

Review Questions

Check that students can name the correct starting squares.

Where does the white king start?

The white king starts on e1.

Where does the black queen start?

The black queen starts on d8.

What is the memory rule for queens?

Queens begin on their own color: white queen on a light square, black queen on a dark square.

Where do the pawns start?

White pawns start on the 2nd rank. Black pawns start on the 7th rank.
