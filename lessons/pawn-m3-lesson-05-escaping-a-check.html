Escaping a Check

When your king is in check, you must answer the check immediately. There are three basic ways to escape: capture the attacking piece, block the attack, or move the king to a safe square.

CaptureTake the enemy piece that is giving check.
BlockPlace a piece between the attacker and your king.
Run awayMove the king to a safe square.
Choose safelyCheck that the king is no longer attacked after the move.

Module Goal

In Module 3, students learn practical chess words and rules used to win and defend a game. This lesson focuses on how to escape a check.

Lesson focus

2

Three Ways to Escape Check

These are the beginner-friendly methods to remember whenever the king is under attack.

1

Capture the enemy piece

Take the piece that is giving check.

2

Block the enemy piece

Place a piece between the attacker and your king.

3

Run away from the enemy piece

Move the king to a safe square.

3

Capture the Enemy Piece

White escapes check by capturing the black rook with Bxa1.

In this example, the white king is checked by the black rook. To escape check, White captures the rook by moving Bxa1.

Position before White moves

The black rook is checking the white king.

Position after White moves Bxa1

The white bishop captures the black rook on a1.

Captured piece: Black rook
4

Block the Enemy Piece

White escapes check by placing the bishop between the rook and the king with Be1.

In this example, the white king is checked by the black rook. To escape check, White blocks the rook by moving Be1.

Position before White moves

The black rook is checking the white king.

Position after White moves Be1

The white bishop moves to e1 and blocks the rook’s attack.

No piece is captured. The bishop “hides” the king from the rook.
5

Run Away from the Enemy Piece

White escapes check by moving the king away to a safe square with Kh2.

In this example, the white king is checked by the black rook. To escape check, White moves the king by playing Kh2.

Position before White moves

The black rook is checking the white king.

Position after White moves Kh2

The white king moves away to h2.

No piece is captured. The king escapes to a safe square.

Quick Reminder

Use this simple checklist when a king is in check.

1

Capture

Take the piece that is giving check.

2

Block

Put one of your pieces between the attacker and your king.

3

Run away

Move your king to a square where it is safe.

These are the three basic ways to escape a check.
✓

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding after the lesson.

1. What must you do if your king is in check?

You must make a move that gets your king out of check.

2. What does it mean to capture the checking piece?

It means you take the enemy piece that is attacking your king.

3. What does it mean to block a check?

It means you put one of your pieces between the checking piece and your king.

4. What does “run away” mean in this lesson?

It means the king moves to a safe square where it is no longer attacked.
