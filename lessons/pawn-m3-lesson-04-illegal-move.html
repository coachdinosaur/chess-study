Illegal Move

An illegal move is a move that is not allowed in chess. In this lesson, White tries to play Ke3, but the move is illegal because the king moves into check.

Illegal moveLearn that some moves are not allowed by the rules of chess.
King ruleUnderstand that you may never move your king into check.
NotationRead Ke3 as “King to e3.”
CorrectionKnow that an illegal move must be taken back and replaced by a legal move.

Module Goal

In Module 3, students learn chess words that explain how games are won, lost, and corrected. This lesson focuses on illegal moves.

Lesson focus
White tries to move the king from e2 to e3. That move is illegal because the black bishop on c5 attacks e3.
2

What Is an Illegal Move?

An illegal move is any move that breaks the rules of chess.

1

Not allowed

An illegal move is not a legal chess move.

2

Take back the move

If a player makes an illegal move, the player must return to the previous position and make another move.

3

Important king rule

You may not move your king into check.

In this example, Ke3 is illegal because the white king moves into check by the black bishop.
3

The Move: Ke3

This notation means the king moves to the square e3.

Ke3

Read Ke3 as: King to e3.

K King
e3 the square where the king moves
Notation tells us the attempted move, but the move still has to be legal.

5

What Happens After an Illegal Move?

If a player makes an illegal move, that player must go back to the previous position, take back the move, and make another move.

Rule to remember: You may never move your king into check.
Beginner tournament note from the lesson source: if a player makes three illegal moves, that player loses the game.

Review Questions

Use these quick questions at the end of the lesson.

1. What is an illegal move?

An illegal move is a move that breaks the rules of chess.

2. Why is Ke3 illegal in this lesson?

Because the black bishop on c5 attacks e3, so the white king would move into check.

3. Are you allowed to move your king into check?

No. You may never move your king into check.

4. What should happen after an illegal move?

The move should be taken back, the previous position restored, and a legal move made instead.
