Algebraic Notation Examples

Practice reading simple chess moves: Kf2, Nd3, and g5. Learn why pieces use capital letters, why pawns do not use the letter P, and why only one piece moves per turn.

Read Kf2Understand that K means King and f2 is the destination square.
Read Nd3Understand that N means Knight and d3 is the destination square.
Write pawn movesUse only the destination square for pawns, such as g5, e4, and d5.
Move one pieceRemember that one normal chess turn moves only one piece.

What these examples show

Algebraic notation is the chess language used to write moves. In this lesson, each example tells you which piece moves and which square it moves to.

Simple move pattern
Piece letter+destination square= written move

For pawns, the piece letter is skipped, so a pawn moving to g5 is written simply as g5.

2

Example moves

Read each move by identifying the piece letter first, then the destination square.

Kf2

The king moves from f1 to f2.

The capital K means King. The square f2 is where the king moves.

Nd3

The knight moves from c1 to d3.

The capital N means Knight. The square d3 is the destination square.

g5

The pawn moves from g4 to g5.

A pawn move does not begin with P. The move is written as the destination square: g5.

3

Important rule: no letter P for pawns

We do not use the letter P when a pawn moves. For pawns, simply write the square the pawn moves to.

Pawn examples

These are pawn moves written correctly:

Pawn to g5 =  g5 Pawn to e4 =  e4 Pawn to d5 =  d5
4

Piece letters

We use capital letters for pieces. In this lesson, the two piece letters are K and N.

Letters used here

K= King
N= Knight
Remember

Piece letters are capital letters. Pawns have no letter in simple pawn moves.

Kf2Nd3g5

Only one piece per turn

Lesson review

Use these quick checks to review the main ideas from the lesson.

Kf2King to f2.
Nd3Knight to d3.
g5Pawn to g5.
No PPawns use only the destination square.
Quick check: What does N mean in Nd3?

N means Knight. The move Nd3 means the knight moves to d3.

Quick check: How do you write a pawn moving to e4?

Write only the destination square: e4. Do not write Pe4 in this beginner notation rule.

Quick check: Can two pieces move in one normal turn?

No. One normal turn moves only one piece.
