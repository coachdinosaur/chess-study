How to Win a Chess Game

We will study basic words and ideas that help us understand how to win a chess game. The first important idea is capture.

1

Module Goal

To win a chess game, you need to understand important chess words and ideas. In this lesson, we begin with the idea of capturing.

Key idea
A capture happens when you remove an opponent’s piece or pawn from the chessboard.
2

What Is a Capture?

A capture happens when a chess player moves one of their own pieces to a square occupied by an opponent’s piece and removes that opponent’s piece from the chessboard.

Beginner definition

When you capture, your chessman takes the place of the opponent’s chessman. The opponent’s chessman is taken off the board.

In this lesson, White captures Black’s pawn.
3

How a Capture Works

A capture has two simple parts.

1
Move to an occupied square

A capture happens when your piece or pawn moves to a square where an opponent’s chessman is standing.

2
Remove the opponent’s chessman

After the move, the opponent’s chessman is taken off the board.

Capture only works if the move is legal for that piece or pawn. In this example, the white pawn captures diagonally from d4 to e5.
4

The Move: dxe5

This move means that a pawn from the d-file captures something on e5.

dxe5

Read dxe5 as: pawn from the d-file captures on e5.

d the pawn from the d-file
x captures
e5 the square where it lands

Review Questions

Use these questions to check understanding.

1. What is a capture?

A capture is when you move to a square occupied by an opponent’s chessman and remove that opponent’s chessman from the board.

2. In the example, what move does White play?

White plays dxe5.

3. What does the letter d mean in dxe5?

It means the pawn came from the d-file.

4. What does x mean in chess notation?

The symbol x means captures.

5. What piece is captured in this lesson?

White captures Black’s pawn on e5.
