Module Goal

Module 3 teaches the words students need in order to understand how a chess game is won. After learning capture and attack, the next important word is check.

Key idea
Check is a special kind of attack because the attacked piece is the king.
2

What Is Check?

A check happens when a piece attacks the enemy king.

CHECK

The king is not captured. Instead, the king is said to be checked or under check.

For this beginner lesson, remember: pieces can give check to the enemy king, but the king itself is not used as the checking piece.
3

Chess Words

These two phrases mean that the king is being attacked.

checked The king is being attacked.
under check The king is in danger and the player must respond.
4

Important Ideas

Check is an attack, but it has special rules because the king is involved.

1
Check is an attack

The enemy king is being attacked by one of your pieces.

2
The king is special

The king is never actually captured. A player must answer the check.

Review Questions

Use these quick questions to check understanding.

1. What is check?

Check is an attack on the enemy king.

2. Is the king actually captured during check?

No. The king is never actually captured. The player must answer the check.

3. In the diagram, which piece gives check?

The black bishop gives check.

4. What diagonal does the bishop attack along?

The bishop attacks along the diagonal b4–c3–d2–e1.
