Delete Your Account.

You can delete your Endgame Trainer account at any time. This page explains how deletion works, what is removed, and what is kept.

Last updated: September 2026

On this page

01

Delete Inside the App

  1. Open Account & Settings in the app.
  2. Open your profile (the account area showing your linked email).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by ticking the permanence acknowledgment and typing DELETE.

Deletion is immediate. You are signed out on this device, and the account cannot be restored afterward.

02

Deletion Without App Access

If you cannot use the app, email cddigital2022@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject line “Endgame Trainer account deletion request.”

Manual requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days of receipt. We may reply to the same address to confirm the request before deleting.

03

What Is Deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, password credential, and Google Sign-In linkage.
  • All sessions — you are signed out everywhere immediately.
  • Your training progress and performance records.
  • Password-reset and email-verification records.
  • Your saved free-puzzle unlock selections and any coaching-session entitlements.
  • Your poll responses and your device-identity linkage.
  • Community feedback you submitted (kept only in anonymized form, if operationally needed).
  • Tournament display names are replaced with “Deleted Player”; the account identity is removed.

04

What Is Retained, and Why

  • Billing reconciliation records — Google Play purchase tokens with the account linkage removed. Google remains the controller of Play purchase data; we keep the minimum needed to reconcile payments and handle refunds or revocations.
  • Anti-abuse identifiers — pseudonymous hashes that prevent deleted accounts from re-claiming the free trial or limited promotional offers. These hashes cannot be reversed into personal data.
  • Anonymized tournament records — games and results involving other players are kept intact so their tournaments remain correct; your name shows as “Deleted Player.”

05

Google Play Subscriptions Are Separate

Deleting your Endgame Trainer account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription through Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to prevent future billing.
