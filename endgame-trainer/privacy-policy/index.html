Privacy Policy.

Coach Dinosaur Endgame Trainer (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chess Endgame Trainer mobile app for Android.

Last updated: March 2026

01 Your data is not sold

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

02 Google Sign-In is optional

It is used only when you choose it for optional online features.

03 Collection is limited

We collect account, profile, and basic technical data needed to run and improve the app.

01

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Email address Used for account creation, login, optional Google Sign-In, and subscription management.
  • Username or display name Used for your in-app profile.
  • Self-reported chess skill level Optional and used only to personalize your training.

We also automatically collect limited technical data such as device type, operating system version, IP address, crash reports, and basic app usage statistics to help us improve performance and fix bugs.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Personalize chess training and track your progress.
  • Process subscriptions and send payment confirmations.
  • Improve the app and resolve technical issues.
  • Respond to your support requests.
We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

03

Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In only when you choose to authenticate with a Google account for optional online features. We do not use Firebase for authentication, analytics, or crash reporting.

04

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. All data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

05

Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for chess learners of all ages. For users under 13, we recommend parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect more personal information than is strictly necessary to run the app.

06

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
